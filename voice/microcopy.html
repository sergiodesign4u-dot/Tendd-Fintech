On the cancel line, and the three things it does not say. No discount, no "are you sure you want to lose", no "tell us why". A product whose whole promise is that cancelling should be easy cannot make its own subscription the hard one, and every retention pattern the category uses here is the pattern node 4.9 exists to help people survive. The consequence is stated above the action rather than sprung after it, and what it says first is what the person is actually afraid of: the list stays.

Two rows are missing from this state on purpose. There is no "What Tendd tells you about" block, because there is no address to send anything to, and a preference screen for messages that cannot arrive is theatre. That fact is not hidden: it is the second of the three lines above, written as what an account changes rather than as a lack. And there is no "Sign out", because there is nothing to sign out of.

Discrepancies flagged

Nothing below is rewritten. These are the places where the product says the same thing in different words, or slips into a tone Tendd said it would avoid. Step 3 (Dictionary and Forbidden) and Step 4 (Microcopy rules) decide each one. See Voice: Dictionary and Voice: Forbidden.

D1. The manual-add action has 7 different labels

The single job "add a subscription by hand" is written many ways. Needs one canonical button label. Resolved by the Dictionary.

Add them yourself Add them yourself instead Add subscription Add it manually + Add subscription Add a subscription Add manually Add another by hand

D2. The connect-bank action has 3 labels

Connect your bank (path-choice, connect-bank, home-empty) vs Choose your bank (connect-bank primary button) vs Connect a bank (connections, connections-empty).

D3. "Go back to Home" is the same destination under 4 names

All of these land on Home.

Back to your subscriptions (alerts-empty, alerts-error) Back to Home (subscription-detail-error, history-trends-empty) Done, back to my list (cancel-win, share-snapshot, share-snapshot-error) See my list / See my full list (add-subscription, guided-reveal).

D4. The retry action has 4 labels

Try again (connect-bank-error, home-error, alerts-error, subscription-detail-error, share-snapshot-error) vs Check again (connect-bank-empty) vs Search again (add-subscription-empty) vs Try the list again (add-subscription-error).

D5. The tracked thing is named 5 ways

The Dictionary must fix one term per concept and say when "service" (the catalog entry) legitimately differs from "subscription" (the thing being tracked). The catalog of presets is presets on welcome but services everywhere else.

subscription (most screens) thing (guided-reveal: "subscribed to 14 things") service (add-subscription: "Find a service", "400+ services", "Loading services") recurring charge (welcome, connect-bank, data-privacy) what you pay for (connections, home-empty).

D6. The bank-or-manual origin is named 3 ways

One dictionary term needed, with "connection" reserved for the Plaid link specifically if we keep both.

source (connections: "Your sources", "Add a source"; settings; data-privacy) connection ("Bank connection via Plaid", "bank connections") bank / account.

D7. The read-only trust line drifts across 4 forms

Two variables move independently: "we" vs "Tendd", and whether "Read-only" leads. This is Tendd's most important trust sentence and should be one fixed line.

Read-only, we can never move your money. (welcome, path-choice) We can never move your money. (connect-bank, data-privacy) Read-only. Tendd cannot move your money. (home, connections) Read-only, cannot move money (connections access value).

D8. "Plaid" takes 3 prepositions

Pick one.

Powered by Plaid (connect-bank) via Plaid (connections) through Plaid (connections, welcome).

D9. The monthly total concept is phrased 4 ways

Choose one label for the headline number.

monthly total (welcome, home-empty) recurring spend (history-trends header) monthly recurring total (history-trends summary) recurring money (welcome benefit card).

D10. Loading screens are inconsistent

Most loaders use one calm sentence ending "This usually takes a moment." (home-loading, alerts-loading, history-trends-loading, share-snapshot-loading). But two use a terse gerund title with an ellipsis: Loading services... (add-subscription-loading) and Creating your card... (share-snapshot-loading). The ellipsis + "-ing..." pattern reads generic and is the one place loaders drift from the calm sentence. The Microcopy rule (Step 4) should settle the loading pattern.

D11. Contractions are used inconsistently

The clearest clash: You're paying for 14 subscriptions (home) vs You are subscribed to 14 things (guided-reveal), the same statement, one contracted and one not, with a different noun on top of it. Voice needs one rule on contractions.

Contracted in places: You're paying Can't find it? Couldn't cancel? Let's try the card again. Spelled out elsewhere: We could not cannot did not It is That is.

D12. Celebratory / hype tone (candidates for the Forbidden list)

Design principle 5 says the cancel moment is the one place a small win is allowed, so this is a deliberate-versus-drift call for Step 3, not an automatic delete. See Voice: Forbidden.

Nice. opener and A small win (cancel-win); Feeling good about it? (cancel-win share); It felt great. (welcome testimonial, quoted, so lower priority).
