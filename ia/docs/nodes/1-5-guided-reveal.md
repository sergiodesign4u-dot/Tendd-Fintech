## Node, type, scope

**Node 1.5 · Guided Reveal · page with three internal steps · MVP**

## Purpose and jobs

The moment the product is built to test. Growth zone 4 says the all-or-nothing reveal is what
confirms an avoider's belief that looking makes things worse, and **H0**, the riskiest
assumption of the whole project, is whether a gradual reveal changes that.

- **J-MAIN** (primary). This is where the main job first completes.
- Realizes **D1**: the reveal is gradual and every step is paired with an action.

## URL and breadcrumbs

`/start/reveal`. In from node 1.3 or node 1.4. Out to node 2.6, which is the same material at
rest.

## Content blocks, mobile-first priority

From `../blocks.md`, type B. The three steps are one page, not three routes: the person never
loses the thread, and back never means starting over.

## Filters and facets

None.

## Primary CTA

**"See my list"**, to node 2.6. One action at the emotional peak; a menu of choices there is a
second decision nobody asked for.

## Emotional support

- **E1 feel competent, not judged** -> money is framed as "what you signed up for", never as
  spending or waste; no score, no red, no advice; the biggest thing on screen is one number the
  person can act on -> **where exactly:** the caption under the total in step three, and the
  fact that steps one and two arrive before any number at all.

This node is the first of the three the table names for E1, and it is the one that carries H0.

## Responsive

Mobile: one step per viewport, the person scrolls or taps forward through three. Desktop: the
same three steps in the same order, never collapsed into one view, because the pacing **is**
the design.

## SEO

**noindex, no schema.** Private, and it renders personal data.
