Landing stats

Four figures the page already says, set large on a recessed panel and counted up from zero as the panel comes into view.

Organism .lp-stats 0 grey pages grid: two columns / one at 40rem added 2026-09-26 with Light 2 (P16)

What it is

Nothing here is new copy, and that is its rule. The example total, the 400+ services, the three steps, and what a year of Pro costs a month: each figure and each sentence under it is one the page says elsewhere. The block exists to let the numbers be seen at the size a number deserves, not to add claims.

The count-up is the page script's and the markup is complete without it: the server renders the final figures ($192.90, 400+, 3, $5.75) and the script counts to them from data-to. With no script, and on this stand, they simply stand.

Behaviour on width

One container point, 40rem: two columns become one.

Limits

Two figures are money and both are examples or prices, never the person's. The $192.90 carries "Example, not your data" beside it on this block too, because a stranger's page must never read as a statement about the reader. $5.75 is D4 as amended on 2026-09-30: $69 a year over twelve months.
