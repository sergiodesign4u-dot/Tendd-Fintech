# Backlog - what this stage found and deliberately did not fix

Opened at the molecule round of step 5, earlier than the pack schedules it, for one reason: the
findings were arriving faster than the steps that own them, and a finding with no home is a
finding that gets lost. Stages 09 to 13 read this and close what their own material closes.

**Reconciled against the product on 2026-08-12**, after the central sweep of step 6, by the second
control census. Every row below carries its state as measured on that date rather than as written
when it was found: **closed**, **open with an owner**, or **corrected**, where the finding held and
the evidence under it did not.

**Nothing open here is a defect on a shipped screen.** One was, for part of 2026-08-12, and it is
recorded in the new section at the foot with its measurement and its repair rather than deleted:
this file exists to be trusted, and a defect that is written down only while it is unfixed teaches
the next reader that the file is a to-do list rather than a record. Everything else is either a
decision that needs a person, or work whose correct owner is a later step. Each row says which.

## What is deliberately NOT in this file

Anything already fixed **and closed by the second census**, anything recorded in
`docs/tokens-audit.md` as one of the four named sources of a visual change, and anything a later
step is scheduled to do anyway. A backlog that lists the plan alongside the exceptions stops being
readable, and a backlog nobody reads is the same as no backlog.

**Closed rows are kept and marked rather than deleted**, which is a change of practice from the
first draft of this file. A row that vanishes on the day it is fixed leaves the next reader unable
to tell a solved problem from one nobody ever found, and this round produced three cases where the
fix had already landed and the document still described it as pending.
