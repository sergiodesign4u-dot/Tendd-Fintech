Landing steps

The "How Tendd works" section: three cards on one hairline path, and inside each card the product itself at a small size, doing the step the words under it describe.

Organism 1 grey page 2 coloured pages added 2026-08-15

What it is

The taste decision is one sentence: show the product small, not an icon that stands for it. The reference sweep (Refero: Wealthsimple, Cake Equity, GoFundMe, TidyCal and the pattern space around them) says the step sections that read as expensive are the ones whose pictures are true. This product already owns every atom needed to be true at thumbnail size: the marks, the skeleton grammar, the chip. So step 1 is three real provider marks and a plus tile (the two doors of D2); step 2 is a list forming - one full row and two skeleton rows, the product's own loading grammar, which is also this page's whole first act said again; step 3 is one row going quiet under the canonical Cancelled chip.

No new strings, no invented prices, no icons, no petrol. The three vignettes are aria-hidden and carry not one word the product does not already own: the chip label is voice/docs/microcopy.md's own Cancelled, reused and not rewritten. Petrol keeps its three jobs - nothing in a vignette is an action, a selection or the trust line, so nothing in a vignette is petrol. The marks' colours are the providers' own, legal here for the same reason they are legal in the list and the aperture: they are content.

The path is the cards' own top edge, continued. Each marker sits ON the top border of its card, and between cards a hairline continues at exactly that height, so the three read as stations on one line rather than three boxes with numbers in them. The intro already promises "three steps, a few minutes"; the geometry now says the same sentence. The marker's own geometry (26px, hairline box, meta, muted) still belongs to numbered steps, whose file prescribes exactly this split: it owns the marker, hosts own its placement.

Anatomy

  • .lp-steps the grid modifier that draws the path: one hairline across its own top at the height of the cards' top borders. The cards are opaque and sit on it, so the line shows only in the gaps, which is precisely where a connection lives
  • .lp-step the card, re-declared upward from the base card: 24 of padding against 16, 32 above for the marker on its edge, the shadow, and a 3px hover lift with no colour change - calm products move, they do not light up
  • .snum the marker, riding the card's top edge at -13px with the surface behind it so the border does not strike through the numeral. Geometry from numbered-steps.css, placement from here
  • .sdemo the vignette well: recessed, radius-wash, one fixed height (148px) for all three so the headings under them stand on one line across the section. A height and not a min-height, because the well is a frame for a fixed picture, not a container for flowing text
  • .drow a vignette row: mark, bar, sometimes a chip. The second and third rows stand at 0.55 and 0.3, which is the deck's own depth grammar at thumbnail size

Limits

The entrance is the page's own grammar and one literal is knowingly repeated. Each card rises on its own view() timeline with cover ranges (an entry range against the element's own height is a pop; cover is a rise), offset left to right so the path is read in order. The curve is the same expo-out landing-orbit.css declares and cannot be read from here - it lives on that component's subtree - so the literal carries its origin in a comment and the backlog carries the debt; stage 11 makes it one token. The reduced-motion kill is by name, because base.css's global guard zeroes durations and a scroll-driven animation has no duration to zero.

The grey changed structure, and the decision is written where the rule demands. The wireframe is frozen; a founder's decision of 2026-08-15 redesigned this section, and the decision is written as a comment on the section in wireframes/index.html. The grey draws the same vignettes in its own greyscale vocabulary - boxes and bars in _wf.css - so the structure contract and the colour stay one drawing at two fidelities. Not one word of the section's copy moved.

What was rejected: icons, screenshots, and a fourth step. An icon set would be the first one in the product and would say less than the atoms already say; a real screenshot in a card repeats the mistake the hero corrected on 2026-08-14 ("все это смотрится очень плоско" - a screenshot of the argument instead of the argument); and the three steps are the sitemap's own three, not a layout choice this component may edit.
