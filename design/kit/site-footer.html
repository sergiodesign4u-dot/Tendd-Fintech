Site footer

The public page's foot: what Tendd is in one sentence, and three columns of links. It exists on exactly one page, because it is the only page a person arrives at from outside.

Organism 1 grey page 1 coloured page form: stacked / four columns added 2026-08-14

What it is

It is not the app's foot. .act-foot is a pattern that closes a task with an action row and a way out. This closes a page, and it carries no action at all. The reason it carries none is the reason the pricing block is not a comparison table: the one action of this page is stated three times already, and a fourth in the footer would be asking after the argument has ended.

The wordmark stands here and the mark does not. D-Brand puts the mark at 22px in the app bar and as the favicon, and a second mark four thousand pixels down the page is a logo asking to be noticed rather than chrome framing a page. The word alone closes it - with the last letter petrol, which D-Brand says holds everywhere, with no condition, and which the grey wrote as plain text.

Anatomy

  • .fbrand the wordmark and one sentence. The widest column and it leads, because a person who scrolled this far without deciding is being told once more, plainly, what the thing is
  • .fcol h4 the column head: caps, tracked, muted. Deliberately not the eyebrow atom - .k is a block mark inside content and this is a column head inside chrome. Same treatment, two jobs, and the day the eyebrow changes the footer should not follow it
  • .fcol a 44px each, because a footer is where a thumb goes hunting

Behaviour on width

One point, the registered 47.5rem, where the four columns stand in a row with the brand column taking 40%.

Limits

No action, by decision. See above: the page's one action is in the bar, the hero and the closing block, and that is three times. A footer button would be a fourth ask after the argument has ended.

Where it comes from

Drawn by design/system/components/site-footer.css through ../system/index.css, the same entry point a product screen loads. It stands on the coloured landing and on wireframes/index.html, and on nothing else: the landing is the only public surface this product has. Registered in docs/inventory.md and in _nav.js, in the organism group.
