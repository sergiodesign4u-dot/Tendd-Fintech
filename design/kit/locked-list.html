Anatomy

Synced from the product, 2026-09-30 (P31, P34). Two objects in one file, and since 2026-09-29 only one of them stands on a screen. The block, .locked-note, is the one place the withheld rows appear: a free scan keeps no name it does not show (P31), so a row would be a placeholder for nothing, and the figures at the top of the screen already count them. The row, .row.is-locked with its slot, is still in the css and is shown further down as what it is: a form the file draws and no screen wears.

The block speaks the public page's language (P34, the founder: "как на хоумпейдж, там более визуально красиво"): the dotted eyebrow, the count as the stat card's figure, the row of squares, the hero's floating tiles as a small stage, the landing's shadows and 24px radius, and its pill buttons with the travelling arrow. Past the desktop point on a wide screen the words stand left and the stage right; on a phone, and on the reveal at every width, the stage comes first.

When to use it

Only where the rows are really there and really counted. That is the whole honesty of the object: the total above it includes every unnamed row, the category counts include them, and the sum line says so in so many words. A block over rows that were excluded from the total would be the product inventing a number to sell the answer to, which is the frame voice.md forbids by name.

It is never the first thing on a screen. D-Gate put the gate inside step 2 of the reveal and on a populated Home, both times after the count and the total have already been given away. A lock at the door is a paywall pretending to be a product, and the founder chose this shape over marking the door Pro precisely so that a person sees something true before they are asked for anything.

And it is a state and never a screen. D3 as amended on 2026-08-20 says the plans are one product, not two, with no Pro twin of a screen. There is no locked Home; there is Home, ending in this block. The moment the rows are named, the block is simply not drawn, and no route changes.

  • .w40 .w55 .w70 an uneven set, and it is load bearing. Identical bars down a column read as redactions of exactly equal length, which is the one shape that says the product is hiding rather than gating. Real merchant names are not the same length

One form, three widths, no other axis. There is no small locked row and no loud one. The three width classes are not sizes, they are a way of not looking like a redaction, and which one a row gets is arbitrary by design: nothing about the withheld name may be inferable from the bar, including its length.

The rule, and the anti-rule

The server sends no name, and since P31 it stores none: a free scan clears the name of every row it withholds straight after the read. The amount and the date are true, and there is nothing behind the bar to uncover.

Never a skeleton

A skeleton pulses, and a bar that breathes says "wait" to a person who has nothing to wait for. Loading and locked must not look the same: one resolves by itself in a second and the other never resolves without a decision. That is why --bg-withheld exists beside --bg-placeholder at the same value.

Limits

  • one slow float Synced from the product, 2026-09-30 (P34). This read "no motion", and it was true of the row and of the first note. The block now floats its three tiles and three chips, one move each, 6s and 7s, alternate, 8px. Reduced motion stops it through base.css like every other animation. The withheld bar itself still never moves: its token comment carries why
  • petrol, counted Synced from the product, 2026-09-30 (P34). This read "no petrol on the row", and on the row it still holds. The block spends petrol on the primary pill (the first job), and borrows the landing's figure, its dotted eyebrow, its field and its front tile, which is the founder's decision in P34 and the landing's own treatment. It is the one place inside the app that does, and it is the place the product sells
  • the count, in words the number of withheld rows is said once, in the heading, as a sentence. It is never a badge beside the list: decision N7 settled what a counter does to this audience, a counter is a demand and a dot is an offer
  • the slot is not a control no hover, no focus, no press on a row or a tile. The way to Pro is the button in the block, once, so that a person cannot arrive at a paywall by poking at their own data
  • U1 one filled action per zone: the block is a zone and carries one .btn.primary; "Add them yourself" is the outline pill beside it
  • U17 a screen-level order belongs to the layout that needs it: the block's order: 3 is scoped .app > .screen:has(> .groups) in app-shell.css, never on the block itself. Usage rules
