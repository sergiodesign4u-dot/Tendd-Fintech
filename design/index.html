Trust and security

Trusted with your money

Trust is earned, not claimed. Here is exactly what Tendd can and cannot do.

Read-only, always

Tendd can see your recurring charges but cannot move, spend, or touch your money.

Bank connection through Plaid

You sign in on your bank's own site, through Plaid, the same service many major finance apps use. We never see your bank password.

You are in control

Disconnect any account or delete all of your data at any time, in one tap. It is gone when you say so.

We never sell your data

Your financial life is yours. Tendd does not sell or share it, full stop.

Questions people ask first

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You can add your subscriptions yourself from 400+ services and get the same calm view. Connecting a bank is one of two ways in, not the price of entry.

Is connecting my bank safe?

You sign in on your bank's own site, through Plaid, so Tendd never sees your password. The connection is read-only. Tendd cannot move your money, and you can disconnect at any time.

What does Tendd cost?

The calm view is free, with no cap on how many subscriptions or banks you add. Tendd Pro is $9.99 a month or $69 a year, and it adds history, trends, and advanced alerts.

Can Tendd cancel subscriptions for me?

Tendd shows you how to cancel, step by step, and that part is free. It does not cancel on your behalf, because that would mean asking for more than read-only access to your money.
