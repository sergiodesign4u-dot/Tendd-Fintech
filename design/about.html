About Tendd

A calm way to see and control your recurring payments. Built for people who are not into finance.

Why it exists

Subscriptions are easy to start and easy to forget, and looking at all of them at once can feel like an audit. Tendd is the opposite of an audit: one list of what you have signed up for, with the monthly total as the biggest thing on the screen.

What it will not do

  • It can never move your moneyThe connection has no permission to, and we never ask for one.
  • It does not sell your dataNot to advertisers, not to anyone, on any plan.
  • It does not judgeNo budgets to set up, no red warnings, no lectures. Just your numbers in plain language.

Who runs Tendd

Tendd is run by one person and not by a company. There is no business registered behind it yet, and when there is, this page will name it.
