Privacy

What Tendd holds about you, who else can see it, and how to take it all back.

Last updated 29 September 2026.

  • We read your recurring chargesRead-only, and nothing else you do with your money.
  • We can never move your moneyThe connection has no permission to, and we never ask for one.
  • We do not sell your dataNot to advertisers, not to anyone, on any plan.

What Tendd holds

Your email address
how you sign in, and where a sign-in link is sent
Your subscriptions
the name, the amount, how often it repeats and when it is next due
Where each one came from
the bank you connected, or the fact that you typed it in
The line your bank prints
the text as it appears on your statement, kept unchanged so the same charge is recognised next month
Your settings
your currency, your plan, and which alerts you want
If you pay for Tendd Pro
the plan you chose, its price, when it renews or ends, and whether the last payment went through, as Stripe reports them. Never the card
The emails Tendd sent you
which alert went out and when, so that none is sent twice

Tendd does not hold your name, your address, your card numbers or your bank login. It never sees them.

Who else can see it

Supabase
holds the database
Resend
sends your sign-in email, the emails about your subscriptions that you choose in Settings, and our replies when you write to us, so it sees your address and what the email says
Cloudflare
checks that a person and not a script is asking for a sign-in link, so it sees your browser and your internet address for that moment; and if you write to privacy@tendd.co, it passes your letter on to the Gmail inbox where it is read
Stripe
takes the payment for Tendd Pro, on its own page, so it sees your card, your email and the billing address it asks for. Tendd learns only the plan and whether the payment went through
Plaid
makes the bank connection and reads recurring charges. We ask Plaid for transactions and for nothing else
Google
only if you sign in with Google, and then it tells us your email address and nothing more
Vercel
serves the pages, and keeps the ordinary record of requests that a web server keeps
Google Fonts
the typeface is loaded from Google, so Google sees your address when a page opens. We plan to serve it ourselves

That is the whole list. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind in Tendd.

What your browser keeps

One cookie, and it is the one that keeps you signed in. Whether you chose light or dark is kept in your browser too, and that never reaches us.

What you can do about it

Download everything Tendd holds for you. It is free, it is yours, and it is on Data and privacy.

Disconnect a bank, on Your sources. The standing permission is withdrawn at Plaid and the key we held is deleted. The subscriptions already on your list stay there until you remove them.

A free bank scan reads once. Tendd then keeps the connection for seven days without reading it, so Tendd Pro can name what the scan found without asking your bank again, and after that it ends by itself. You can end it sooner on Your sources.

Delete everything, and your account goes with it. There is no archive and no copy kept back.

How long Tendd keeps it

Until you delete it. Nothing here expires on its own, because a list you have kept for two years is the reason to keep a list.

When this page changes

The date at the top is the day it last changed. If Tendd ever holds something new, or hands your data to someone not named above, this page will say so.

Who runs Tendd

Tendd is run by one person and not by a company. There is no business registered behind it yet, and when there is, this page will name it.

Write to privacy@tendd.co about anything on this page: what Tendd holds about you, how to get a copy of it, how to remove it, or a sentence here that does not match what you see. One person reads that address.
