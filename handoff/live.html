Tendd - Handoff

The live product, since v1.0

This repository was handed over on 2026-08-23 and frozen. The product built from it went live at tendd.co and kept changing by the founder's decisions. On 2026-09-30 every page here was brought level with it. This is the account of that sync.

synced at product commit 4158f6c 73 pages in colour, was 58 75 components + 3 patterns, was 70 + 3 22 decisions logged in the product since 2026-09-02 28 gaps open of 55

How it was done

The running product was the reference, not its source code read in isolation. The product's own development server was walked by a script as eleven people built for it in its development database, one per shape of data the design draws: a list typed by hand, the free bank scan held for seven days, the same scan after the hold ended, Tendd Pro with a live bank, Pro cancelled but not ended, Pro with a failing renewal, a bank that needs a new sign-in, an empty list, one subscription, a short list, and a cancellation reported. Every route was captured as rendered markup and as screenshots at 390 and 1440, and every page in design/ was compared with the capture of its state.

What travelled whole, copied rather than rewritten: design/system/ (byte for byte the product's system/), voice/docs/microcopy.md, docs/decisions.md (with a mirror header), docs/gaps.md (new here), docs/bank-connection.md, handoff/docs/behaviour.md and the fourteen merchant marks in design/visuals/logos/. The landing's motion is the product's own script, bundled into design/landing.js, with the command that rebuilds it in its banner.

The one rule every page was brought level by. What the product DECIDED changes the page: a structure, a class, a string or a state that moved because of a decision with an id. What the product has simply not built yet stays as the design's intent, because a gap in a running product is not a verdict on the design. Every changed page says which it was in its own top comment, with the decision ids.
