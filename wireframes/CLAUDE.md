# wireframes/ - what breaks forever if you get it wrong

1. **Never color anything here.** Grey is the contract, not a stage of progress. Color goes onto
   copies in `design/`, which may differ from their grey original by styling only.
2. **Structure and text belong to this folder.** States, blocks, order and copy are decided here,
   from the IA and the voice, never in `design/`.
3. **`_wf.css` is the single grey stylesheet.** No page level look, no brand, no icons.
4. **`index.html` is the product home page**, the Welcome landing, node 1.1. The hub with every
   screen and state is `overview.html`. Never swap the two.
5. **One page per state**, each with a real way out, and at 360 the screen fills the viewport.
