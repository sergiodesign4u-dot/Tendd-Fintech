Connect your bank

Next you'll pick your bank and sign in on your bank's own site. Tendd never sees your password, and you choose which accounts to share.

How long it takes
About a minute
What you need
Your online banking login, entered on your bank's site

Bank data needs somewhere that is yours: a place you can sign back into, and a place you can tell us to delete. We'll send a link to confirm it, and you can carry on now.

  • What Tendd can seeThe charges that repeat on your account, and nothing else you do with your money.
  • What Tendd can never doRead-only. Tendd cannot move your money.
  • What you can undoDisconnect at any time, and your bank data goes with it.
